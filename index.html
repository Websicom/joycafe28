Our story

Welcome to Joy

We've put so much love into creating a space that feels warm, relaxed and a little bit special.

For us, joy is found in the little things, the moments that make life brighter. We hope our cafe brings a little joy to your day.

Our vision? Healthy by day, naughty by night. A cafe and wine bar where you'll find nourishing food, simple plates, good coffee, cheese, wine and the things we generally love to eat and drink. And, of course, great company and good vibes.

Created with joy, to bring you joy.
Steff & Mike

Joy sits alongside ESSE, the much-loved women's fashion, gifts and therapy destination next door. Steff spent many years working there, so opening Joy feels like a natural new chapter within a community already very close to the family.
